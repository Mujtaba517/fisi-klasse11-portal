## Flagged while reading AEuP11

- The printed ER section allows attribute ovals on entities and on relationships. A handwritten margin that seemed to limit relationship attributes to n:m is not part of the printed section and is not taught as a rule. The lesson says so.
- A handwritten note that the key attribute “is text” is not taught as a data-type rule. The lesson teaches the printed mark: underline exactly one key per entity.
- The Schüler / Klasse drawing asks the two cardinality questions and does not print the numbers. The lesson does not invent those numbers. The worked numbers are the printed ones: Auftrag/Rechnung 1:1, Abteilung/Mitarbeiter 1:n, Artikel/Lieferant n:m.
- Page 18 is almost empty.
- Exercise pages contain sample personal data and a solution sketch. They are not copied.
- Other notebooks contain WLAN access data and name watermarks. None of that is in the lessons or in this file.
